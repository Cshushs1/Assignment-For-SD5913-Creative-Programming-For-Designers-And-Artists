## One AI Suggestion I Kept

One idea I kept from my discussion with ChatGPT was the distinction between making something work and actually understanding how it works.

This connected strongly with my own experience building a phone case website with AI-assisted coding. I was able to make the website work very quickly, but I could not fully explain how functions such as language switching and currency conversion worked.

I kept this idea because it helped me express a question I had already experienced personally:

> If I cannot understand a system, can I really say that I designed it?

This became an important part of my argument about why learning programming still matters to me.
